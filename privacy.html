Privacy notice

IDTA Revision is an unofficial, student-made study app for the Intelligent Data & Text Analytics module. It is run by the class organiser (the person who shared the link with you), not by the university.

What we store

DataWhy
Name, mobile number, email, university, courseTo create your account, log you in with your mobile number, email you password-reset codes, and show your name and course on the leaderboard if you choose
PasswordNever stored. Your browser scrambles it (PBKDF2, 150,000 rounds) and the server stores only a salted hash of that, which can't be turned back into your password
Quiz activity: which questions you answered, right or wrong, topic, mock scores, Calc Lab attemptsTo back up your progress across devices, run the leaderboard, and show the organiser which topics the class finds hard
Saved progress (mistakes list, must-know ticks, mock history)To restore your progress when you log in on another device
Payment screenshot and transaction ID (only if you upload one to unlock the course exams)So the organiser can check your payment and unlock your account. The screenshot is kept in the organiser's private Google Drive and is never shown to other students
Certificates you claim (name, score, exam, date)So anyone you share the certificate link with can check it's genuine

Where it's stored

In a private Google Sheet (and, for payment screenshots, a private Google Drive folder) owned by the class organiser, accessed through Google Apps Script. Only the organiser can open it. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app has no trackers or analytics.

Who can see what

  • Other students see only your name, course and scores on the leaderboard, and only if you leave "Show me on the leaderboard" switched on.
  • The organiser can see your account details and activity in order to run the app.

Your choices

  • Hide yourself from the leaderboard at any time in the Account tab.
  • Delete your account in the Account tab. This permanently removes your details, saved progress and all your activity.
  • Questions or requests: contact the class organiser, or open an issue without including personal details.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account, or until the organiser deletes all data after the module ends.

On your device

The app saves your login and progress in your browser's local storage so it works offline. Logging out or deleting your account clears it from that device.
